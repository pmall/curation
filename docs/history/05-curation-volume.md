# Curation volume, estimated on 2026-10-14

Every three months (the pace of the UniProt releases), about 2,000 new publications go through pre-curation, and about 200 are selected and curated: about 90 % are discarded at pre-curation.

Three months are about 60 work days, so about 4 publications to curate per work day on average. With a margin, a capacity of 20 curated publications per work day gives about 1,200 publications every three months: room for other kinds of publications and for regular re-curation.

Hence the target: a curation or re-curation session handles at most 20 publications.
