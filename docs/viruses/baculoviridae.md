# Baculoviridae

Family conventions: the apoptosis regulators keep the protein names UniProt and the field use (`IAP1`, `IAP3`, `P35`); every other protein is named by its ORF number, written `ORF<number>` (UniProt writes it `ORF-55`, `ORF19` or `Orf120` depending on the entry).
