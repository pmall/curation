# Caliciviridae

Family conventions: the non-structural proteins take the name of their UniProt chain (`NS1`, `NS2`, `NS1-2`, `NTPase`, `VPg`, `3CLpro`, `RdRp`), not the NS1–NS7 numbering, which UniProt gives only as alternative names and only for part of them (NS3 = NTPase, NS5 = VPg, NS6 = 3CLpro, NS7 = RdRp). The capsid proteins keep `VP1` and `VP2`.

Names: the Norwalk 1968 strain (Q83883, Q83885) is the reference; the interactors also come from GII (P54634, Lordsdale), GIV, bovine GIII and murine (Q80J95) strains, whose coordinates differ. `NS1-2` rather than `p48` or `p37`: the chain name, the same in every strain, while the kDa names change with the genogroup.

Unresolved: none. Two fragments are marked `check` in the assignment table: Q80J95 1416–1537 (122 residues inside RdRp, named `RDRP`) and I1ZB53 863–888 (26 residues of VPg).
