# Sedoreoviridae

Family conventions: rotaviruses use the UniProt short names of their segments, `VP1`–`VP6` for the structural proteins and `NSP1`–`NSP5` for the non-structural ones (not the NS26/NS28/NS34/NS53 kDa names); the star of `VP5*` is part of the UniProt name of that cleavage product. Orbiviruses and seadornaviruses use `VP…` and `NS…` as UniProt writes them for each segment.
