# Nimaviridae

Family conventions: white spot syndrome virus proteins are named by the ORF label of their UniProt entry, lowercase `wsv###` as UniProt's entry names write it; an entry numbered in another isolate's series keeps that series (`WSSV312`). No protein of this virus has a Swiss-Prot entry.

Names:
