# Flaviviridae

Family conventions: the non-structural proteins are written `NS1`, `NS2A`, `NS2B`, `NS3`, `NS4A`, `NS4B`, `NS5` (UniProt short names), the structural ones `C`, `prM`, `pr`, `M`, `E` (the letter ending the UniProt chain name). Joined chains take the names of their parts, hyphenated, the second one short: `NS2B-3`, `NS4A-2K`, `2K-NS4B`, `prM-E`. A whole genome polyprotein used as one interactor is `Polyprotein`.
