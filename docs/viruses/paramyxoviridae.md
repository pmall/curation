# Paramyxoviridae

Family conventions: the one-letter gene names, as UniProt and the field use them (`N`, `P`, `M`, `F`, `G`, `H`, `L`, `SH`); the P-gene products each take the letter of their protein name (`P`, `V`, `W`, `C`, `C'`). `F` names the whole F0 precursor.
