# Hantaviridae

Family conventions: as the other bunyaviruses (Phenuiviridae, Peribunyaviridae, Nairoviridae): `N`, `NSs`, `L`; the envelope polyprotein by its UniProt gene `GP`, its mature glycoproteins by their UniProt short names `Gn`, `Gc`.

| Generic name | Protein (UniProt name; gene)                    | Reference     |  Length | Also called                                 |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------: | ------------------------------------------- |
| `N`          | Nucleoprotein; N                                | O36307 1–428  | 314–428 | Protein N, nucleocapsid protein             |
| `NSs`        | Non-structural protein NS-S; N                  | Q80DP8 1–63   |      63 | —                                           |
| `GP`         | Envelopment polyprotein; GP (whole polyprotein) | Q9E006 1–1138 |   1,138 | M polyprotein, glycoprotein precursor (GPC) |
| `Gn`         | Glycoprotein N; GP                              | Q9E006 19–651 | 631–633 | Glycoprotein G1                             |

Names: `GP`, the Swiss-Prot gene, for the uncleaved M polyprotein (named `M` in Drakkar, the segment); the TrEMBL entries call it GPC. `Gn` rather than `G1`: the UniProt short name and the current name in the field.
