# Virgaviridae

Family conventions: the readthrough replicase of the tobamoviruses keeps its Swiss-Prot full name, `Replicase large subunit`: the alternative names are kDa names (182 kDa, 183 kDa) that change with the species, so they cannot serve as one name for the family.

## Youcai mosaic virus (*Tobamovirus youcai*, NCBI 3432897)

| Generic name              | Protein (UniProt name; gene) | Reference     | Length | Also called                                          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------- | -----: | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `Replicase large subunit` | Replicase large subunit      | Q66220 1–1597 |  1,597 | 182 kDa protein, RNA replicase readthrough component |

## Wasabi mottle virus (*Tobamovirus wasabi*, NCBI 3432896)

| Generic name              | Protein (UniProt name; gene)                                                                                       | Reference     | Length | Also called     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------- | -----: | --------------- |
| `Replicase large subunit` | RNA replicase readthrough protein (TrEMBL Q8V9A2; closest Swiss-Prot is Youcai mosaic virus Q66220, 93 % identity) | Q66220 1–1597 |  1,597 | 182 kDa protein |

Names: the three interactors are the same protein of three tobamoviruses, named `RDRP`, `Replicase` and `MT/HEL` in Drakkar; `MT/HEL` names only the two N-terminal domains of a 1,597-residue readthrough protein.
