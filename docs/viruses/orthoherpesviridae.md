# Orthoherpesviridae

Family conventions: each protein keeps the gene or ORF name of its virus, as UniProt writes it: BamHI ORFs in EBV (`BZLF1`), UL/US/RL names in HSV and HCMV (`UL36`), ORFnn and Kn in KSHV and other gammaherpesviruses (`ORF57`, `K15`), ORFnn in VZV, M/m names in MCMV, U names in HHV-6 and HHV-7. Core proteins take that gene name too, not the herpes-wide names (`UL86`, not MCP; `BALF2`, not DBP; `UL31`, not NEC1). Protein names are kept for the envelope glycoproteins (`gB`, `gC`, `gD`, `gE`, `gG`, `gH`, `gI`, `gJ`, `gK`, `gL`, `gM`, `gN`, `gO`, `gQ1`, `gQ2`, `gp42`, `GP350`) and for the regulators known by them: EBV EBNAs and LMPs, HSV ICPs, `VP16` and `VP22` in the alphaherpesviruses, `IE1`/`IE2` and `pp65`/`pp71`/`pp150` in HCMV, `IE4`/`IE61`/`IE62`/`IE63` in VZV, and the KSHV proteins named by UniProt (`LANA1`, `vIRF-1`…, `vFLIP`, `v-cyclin`, `vIL-6`, `vBCL2`, `vGPCR`, `K-bZIP`, `KCP`). Spellings `vFLIP`, `v-cyclin`, `vBCL2` are used for every gammaherpesvirus.
