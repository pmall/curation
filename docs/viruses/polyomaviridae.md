# Polyomaviridae

Family conventions: the early proteins take the UniProt short names `LT`, `ST` and `MT` (large, small and middle T antigen), the capsid proteins `VP1`, `VP2`, `VP3`, and the agnoprotein `Agno` (its UniProt alternative short name). The same names in every species.

Names: VP3 has no Swiss-Prot entry of its own; it is the C-terminal part of VP2, and keeps the name of the TrEMBL entry (P90448, gene VP3) and of the field.
