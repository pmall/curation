# Spinareoviridae

Family conventions: the UniProt short names of the orthoreovirus proteins, with the Greek letter written out and capitalised as UniProt does (`Mu2`, `MuNS`, `Sigma3`, `SigmaNS`), not the segment names (M1, M3, S3, S4). Proteins with no Swiss-Prot entry keep the name of their TrEMBL entry.

| `Mu2`        | Microtubule-associated protein mu-2; M1 | Q00335 1–736 |    736 | mu-2, M1     |
| `MuNS`       | Protein mu-NS; M3                       | Q9PY83 1–721 |    721 | mu-NS, M3    |

| `SigmaNS`    | Protein sigma-NS; S3                    | P07940 1–366 |    366 | sigma-NS, S3 |

Names: strain Lang (type 1) entries are the reference; the Dearing (type 3) interactors are the same proteins. One description calls mu-NS `M3`, its segment name.
