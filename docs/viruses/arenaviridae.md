# Arenaviridae

Family conventions: the UniProt gene names (`N`, `GPC`, `L`, `Z`); the mature glycoproteins by their UniProt short names (`GP1`, and `GP2` when used).

| `GPC`        | Pre-glycoprotein polyprotein GP complex; GPC (whole polyprotein) | P09991 1–498  |    498 | Pre-GP-C, GP-C                  |
| `L`          | RNA-directed RNA polymerase L; L                                 | P14240 1–2210 |  2,210 | Protein L                       |
