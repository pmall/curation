| Generic name | Protein (UniProt name; gene)                    | Reference     | Length | Also called   |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------- | -----: | ------------- |
| `N`          | Nucleoprotein; N                                | P89522 1–482  |    482 | Protein N     |
| `GP`         | Envelopment polyprotein; GP (whole polyprotein) | Q8JSZ3 1–1684 |  1,684 | M polyprotein |
| `L`          | RNA-directed RNA polymerase L; L                | Q6TQR6 1–3945 |  3,945 | Protein L     |

Names: `GP`, the UniProt gene, for the uncleaved M polyprotein (named `M` in Drakkar, the segment). The OTU domain of L (Q6TQR6 1–169, named `vOTU` in Drakkar) has no chain in UniProt: it is a region of `L`, listed to check.
