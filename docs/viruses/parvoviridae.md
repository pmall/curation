# Parvoviridae

Family conventions: the non-structural proteins take their UniProt gene/short name (`NS1`, `NS2`, `NP1`, and for the dependoparvoviruses the four Rep proteins `Rep78`, `Rep68`, `Rep52`, `Rep40`), the capsid proteins `VP1`, `VP2`, `VP3`.
