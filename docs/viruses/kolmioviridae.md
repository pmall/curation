# Kolmioviridae

Family conventions: the two forms of the delta antigen keep UniProt's short names `S-HDAg` and `L-HDAg`, hyphenated, as the large and small envelope proteins of hepatitis B are written `L-HBsAg` and `S-HBsAg` in `viruses.md`.

## Hepatitis delta virus (*Deltavirus italiense*, NCBI 2844074)

| Generic name | Protein (UniProt name; gene) | Reference    |  Length | Also called        |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | ------------ | ------: | ------------------ |
| `S-HDAg`     | Small delta antigen; HDAg    | P0C6L3 1–195 |     195 | p24, SHDAg, HDAg-S |
| `L-HDAg`     | Large delta antigen; HDAg    | P0C6L5 1–214 | 213–214 | p27, LHDAg, HDAg-L |

Names: the Drakkar names `SHDAg` and `LHDAg` (all 492 descriptions) are the same names without the hyphen; UniProt writes `S-HDAg` and `L-HDAg`. The mature chain of the large antigen is 1–211 (the entry is 214 residues); the interactors cover the whole entry.
