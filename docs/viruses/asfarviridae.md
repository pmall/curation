# Asfarviridae

Family conventions: African swine fever virus proteins are named by their ORF name as UniProt writes it (`E199L`, `A238L`, `I215L`), multigene-family members by their family label without the space UniProt puts in "MGF 360-15R" (`MGF360-15R`), and the structural proteins UniProt itself names by molecular weight keep that name (`p22`, `p34`, `p54`, `CD2v`).

Names:

- `I215L` rather than UBCv1 or UBCE2: the ORF name, as for the other proteins of this virus.
- `MGF505-7R` and `MGF360-12L` rather than the strain gene names A528R and Ken-036, so that the multigene family members read alike.
- `CD2v`: the UniProt alternative name the field uses; the entry's only gene is a strain locus tag (Mal-066).
- `p34` is a mature chain of the pp220 polyprotein (Q08358 45–368), not a protein of its own.
