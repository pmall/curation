# Astroviridae

Family conventions: the ORF2 capsid product keeps the UniProt chain name of its species (`VP90` for human and porcine astroviruses, `VP86` for mink astrovirus), its spike product keeps `VP25`; the ORF1 genome-linked protein is `VPg` (UniProt's alternative name for the chain "Viral genome-linked protein", which has no short name).

## Human astrovirus (*Mamastrovirus hominis*, NCBI 3430629)

| Generic name | Protein (UniProt name; gene)                                                                                                              | Reference                 | Length | Also called                   |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -----: | ----------------------------- |
| `VP90`       | Capsid polyprotein VP90; ORF2 (no Swiss-Prot entry of the full-length product: the Swiss-Prot entry O12792 carries its shorter VP70 form) | A0A3G6VE21 1–787 (TrEMBL) |    787 | VP70, capsid precursor        |
| `VP25`       | Spike protein VP25; ORF2                                                                                                                  | Q9IFX1 424–648            |    225 | VP27 (the longer spike chain) |
| `VPg`        | Viral genome-linked protein; ORF1                                                                                                         | P0C6K4 665–755            |     91 | —                             |

Names: `VP90` is the name both TrEMBL entries carry and the name of the uncleaved ORF2 product (787 residues); Swiss-Prot has only the 657-residue VP70 form of the same protein.

## Mink astrovirus (*Mamastrovirus mustelae*, NCBI 3430633)

| Generic name | Protein (UniProt name; gene)  | Reference    | Length | Also called                 |
| ------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------ | -----: | --------------------------- |
| `VP86`       | Capsid polyprotein VP86; ORF2 | Q80KJ6 1–775 |    775 | VP90 (the entry name), ORF2 |

Names: UniProt names the full-length chain of this species VP86 (the entry itself is called "Capsid polyprotein VP90"); the five descriptions call it `ORF2`, a gene name shared by the whole family.
