# Togaviridae

Family conventions: the alphavirus non-structural proteins are written `nsP1`, `nsP2`, `nsP3`, `nsP3'`, `nsP4` (UniProt short names, capital P, prime for the opal read-through product), the structural ones `C`, `E3`, `E2`, `6K`, `E1`. Precursors kept as interactors take their UniProt names: `P123` for the non-structural precursor, `p130` for the whole structural polyprotein, `E3-E2-6K-E1` for the joined envelope chains.
